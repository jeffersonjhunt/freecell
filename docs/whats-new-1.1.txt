Solitaire 1.1

• Scoring — every game starts at 0. Cards to the foundations and finished suits add points; undos and extra passes through the deck take them away. Win, and a time bonus of up to 600 is added — the faster, the more.
• Hard Core — one pass through the deck in Draw 1, three in Draw 3. Turn it on in Settings.
• Your Top 10 for Draw 1 and Draw 3, synced across your devices with iCloud, plus Game Center leaderboards for normal and Hard Core games. Find them under More ▸ Scores.
• Win animations — Cascade, Rainfall, Decay, Shuffle, Random or None, in Settings.
• A new look — orange instead of yellow, a new win card and questions, and More is now a card of tiles.
• Ask before ending a game — turn it off in Settings and New Game or the draw switch never asks.
• Restart — turn on Restart button in Settings to play the same deal again from the start. A restarted game scores 0 and doesn't count toward your Top 10 or Game Center.

Please try: win a game in each draw mode and check it appears in Scores and in Game Center; try each win animation; play a Hard Core game to the end of its passes; restart a game and check its win scores 0 and stays out of Scores. Scores you post while testing are real Game Center entries.
